Hello, I'm Thomas Mercier

After 12 years in the banking and private wealth management sector, I founded Smart Wealth Blog with a simple mission: to make financial education accessible, honest and actionable for 25–45 year-olds.

Too often, the financial advice available online is either too generic or clearly driven by commercial interests (commissions on recommended products). Here, I am committed to full transparency: no product is recommended in exchange for payment.

My Background

I started my career at a major French banking group where I managed private client portfolios with assets between €500,000 and €5 million. This experience gave me a deep understanding of the real mechanics of wealth taxation, financial markets and real estate.

In 2019, I took the leap to independence by setting up my CGPI practice. Since then, I have helped hundreds of individuals build and optimise their wealth. In 2024, I launched this blog to share that expertise on a wider scale.
